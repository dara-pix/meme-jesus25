Almost 2 years ago, just for fun, I was dead set on making a micro-game based on the Jesus meme, and now I’m just as dead set on finally releasing it. I polished it up a bit, and well, here it is

Features: when hovering over the "МОЧА" ("PISS") button, 3D Jesus stretches out his hand with a goblet and says "piss", and when hovering over "ХЛЭБ" ("BREAD"), he reaches out with a loaf of bread and says "bread". You can also play one of the preset background tracks, adjust the volume, or mute the sound.
